
# CIT 140 Portfolio — Cedar Junction Civic Operations

## Overview
This repository contains my CIT 140 coursework for the Cedar Junction Civic Operations case. It documents my work analyzing system requirements and modeling the clinic's current appointment process. Each week's folder contains the files and evidence associated with that assignment.

## Week 4 — What Must the System Do?

Week 4 focused on requirements elicitation, validation, and professional guidance.

**Main work completed:**
- Identified and documented eight system requirements.
- Connected requirements to stakeholder needs and source records.
- Classified requirements by type, including functional, user, business, nonfunctional, and constraint requirements.
- Reviewed the requirements for clarity, necessity, traceability, and checkability.
- Assessed how professional organizations contribute to systems analysis and requirements engineering.

**Files:**
- `CIT140 Week 4 Validated Requirements.pdf` — The final requirements document.
- `README.md` — Week 4 folder notes, if included.

**Location:** Open the Week 4 folder in this repository.

## Week 5 — How Does the Work Happen?

Week 5 builds on the requirements from Week 4 by modeling how the clinic's current appointment process works.

**Main work completed:**
- Created a swimlane process diagram showing the responsibilities of the Visitor, Scheduling, Clinical Operations, Intake, and Next Staff Team.
- Mapped appointment booking, readiness checks, visitor arrival, and exception-handling paths.
- Used the clinic's source records to distinguish the normal appointment path from the incident path.
- Identified the unresolved handoff between booking confirmation and the readiness status displayed to Intake.
- Prepared a Scope and Findings page documenting the process, evidence, uncertainty, and learning reflection.

**Files and locations:**
- `Week05/Artifact/` — Editable diagrams.net process model and exported PDF.
- `Week05/Evidence/` — Screenshot of the Week 5 Discord post.
- `Week05/Receipt/` — Week 5 Mission Receipt PDF.
- `Week05/README.md` — Week 5 folder guide, if included.

## What I Learned
Week 4 helped me understand how to identify, classify, and validate system requirements. Week 5 helped me apply that understanding to a real process model by showing how work moves between roles and where information can become unclear. Comparing the incident record with the normal workflow also showed why evidence must support conclusions and why missing information should be documented rather than assumed.
